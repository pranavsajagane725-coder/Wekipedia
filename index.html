Mahendra Singh Dhoni

From Wikipedia

Mahendra Singh Dhoni
(born 7 July 1981), commonly known as
MS Dhoni
, is an Indian former international cricketer and captain. He is known for his wicket-keeping, finishing ability, and calm leadership.

Early life

Dhoni was born in Ranchi, in the Indian state of Jharkhand. Before focusing on cricket, he played football as a goalkeeper. His athletic background helped him develop quick reflexes and coordination behind the stumps.

Cricket career

He made his international debut for India in 2004. As captain, Dhoni led India in all three major international formats and became known for backing young players and staying composed in close matches.

At domestic level, he has also played for Chennai Super Kings in the Indian Premier League, where he became one of the team's most recognizable players.

Achievements

  • Captained India to victory in the 2007 ICC World Twenty20.
  • Led India to the 2011 Cricket World Cup title.
  • Captained India to the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy title.
